📝 Individual Practice & Guided Workbook Tasks (20 Minutes)

Complete exercises independently in your Student Book and Workbook. Avoid copy-pasting; focus on personal understanding.

Student Book & Workbook Exercises

Open Student Book page 136. Read sentences about past events and rewrite them following the grammar rule. Then complete Workbook page 95.

✅ Answer Key & Explanation:

  • 1. walk ➔ walked (add -ed)
  • 2. dance ➔ danced (add -d because it ends in e)
  • 3. play ➔ played (consonant + y rule doesn't apply to vowel + y)

🤝 Cooperative Teamwork & Role Assignment (30 Minutes)

Work in pairs or small teams. Every student has a specific role to create a concrete product (mini poster, sentence matrix, or dialogue chart).

👥 Team Roles

  • • Speaker: Presents findings in plenary.
  • • Writer: Records team sentences on paper.
  • • Timekeeper: Keeps team on track (Habit 3: Put First Things First).

📋 Collaborative Task

Create 3 past tense sentences about what you did last weekend using today's grammar rule. Practice presenting them together.

Synergize (Habit 6): Combine everyone's ideas to make the most creative sentences!

🌟 Plenary, Interactive Game & Home Research Challenge (15 Minutes)

Mini-presentations by teams, checking understanding, and setting the home investigation task.

🎮 Interactive Digital Game / Trivia

Sample Question: What is the past tense of "listen"? 👉 Listened!

🏠 Home Research Challenge

Ask a family member: "What did you do last weekend?" Write down 2 sentences in your notebook using regular past tense verbs and share them tomorrow.
